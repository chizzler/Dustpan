Task manager · Linux · Mac

Private, free task management

A free, private solution for task management. Dustpan is a native task manager for Linux and Mac. Everything lives on your machine. It works on its own, or syncs through an opensource server you run wherever you like. when you want your other devices in step.

Features

Tasks, Projects, Sections & more

Dustpan has all the modern features a to-do app should have. Heaps of options to customise your workflows. Both keyboard and mouse are first-class citizens, with drag and drop onto a calendar window for rapid date & task length changes.

Projects

Nested as deep as you like

Projects hold sub-projects, sections and tasks. Give each one a colour and an icon, and drag to reorder or nest.

every!

Repeats, repeat

every 3 days runs on the calendar. every! 3 days counts from when you finish.

Quick add

Rapid disclosures

Dates, times, repeats, priority, project, labels and durations, pulled from one line of text.

Subtasks

Micro progress graph

A pie on each parent fills as subtasks are done. Tick the last one and Dustpan offers to finish the parent.

Views

Today, Upcoming, Anytime

Plus Completed, and saved views built from a filter language, like p1 & overdue.

Calendar

Month, Week and Day

Drag tasks onto a day or a time slot. Stretch a block to set how long it takes. Optional keys for every action.

Reminders

Desktop notifications

Fixed times or relative to the due date. They move when the due date moves.

Notes

Markdown on every task

Lists, links and headings in a task's notes, stored as plain text you can read anywhere.

Undo

Nothing is lost by accident

Undo for every edit, and a Trash that keeps deleted tasks and projects for restore or permanent clearance.

Sync

Works stand-alone. Sync to a server if you need.

Add an OpenTodo server to keep every device in sync. Clients keep a full copy of your tasks and work without a connection. Edits merge field by field, so two devices can change one task without losing either change.

Your server

OpenTodo

Open source, one small binary. It holds the log of changes and passes them on.

Privacy

Escape the platform ecosystem clutches.

A task list knows a lot about you: where you're going, who you owe and what's worrying you. Dustpan keeps that between your devices and your server.

No telemetry

No analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts with us. Nothing leaves your machine unless you set up sync.

Your server, your rules

Sync runs through a server you host. Lose it and you lose nothing: every device holds the whole list.

Encrypted in transit

Dustpan refuses plain http to any machine but your own, and won't follow a redirect, so your token can't leak.

A short supply chain

No PyPI packages on Linux and no third-party Swift packages on the Mac. Less code you didn't ask for.

Platforms

Native on every desk.

Each client is written for its own system, not wrapped in a browser. They share one data model and one set of tests.
