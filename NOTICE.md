## OpenTodo

Parts of Dustpan adapt OpenTodo's sync, ordering, recurrence, reminders, dates, filters, quick add,
display and calendar rules, copyright their respective contributors, under AGPL-3.0-only.
The Python implementation and its Swift ports include modifications for native interfaces, local storage,
account handling and offline sync. Initial ports followed v0.18.0; the tested server is v0.22.0.
The quick-add vocabulary and bundled upstream test fixtures are copied from OpenTodo. Linux source
archives include the upstream license with those fixtures. OpenTodo remains a separate project.

## Lucide

Both clients bundle Lucide icon data under the ISC license, including the Feather MIT attribution
recorded in `desktop/dustpan/icons/LICENSE-lucide.txt` and its identical Swift resource copy.
The OpenTodo icon catalogue is copied from OpenTodo. Preserve these notices when distributing the icons.
